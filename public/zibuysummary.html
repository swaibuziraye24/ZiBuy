Platform Overview
What Is ZiBuy?
Uganda's growing online marketplace for buying and selling anything

ZiBuy is a mobile-first classifieds marketplace built for Uganda — connecting buyers and sellers across phones, electronics, vehicles, property, fashion, jobs, services, agriculture, and more. It combines the familiar, no-friction listing experience Ugandans already know from classifieds apps with modern trust and safety systems, real-time communication, and tools that help serious sellers grow — all built around Mobile Money, the way Ugandans actually pay for things.

📱Mobile-First & Installable

Works as a full Progressive Web App — installs to the home screen like a native app, with offline support built in.

🛡️Trust-First by Design

Phone verification, seller trust scores, two-way ratings, and dispute resolution — not bolted on, built into the core.

💬Real Conversations

Live, instant messaging between buyers and sellers with read receipts — no refreshing, no waiting.

💰Built for Mobile Money

Every payment flow — subscriptions, boosts, checkout — is built natively around MTN and Airtel Mobile Money.

Trust & Safety
Trust & Safety Systems
The systems that make ZiBuy feel safe to buy and sell on

📱 Phone (OTP) Verification

Sellers verify their real phone number via a one-time SMS code before earning a "Phone Verified" badge — reducing fake accounts.

⭐ Trust Score System

Every seller has a live-calculated Trust Score (0–100) based on verification status, review history, and account tenure — displayed as New, Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Elite trust tiers.

🌱 New Seller🥇 Gold Trust💎 Elite Trust

🏆 Earned "Trusted Seller" Badge

A top-tier badge that cannot be purchased at any price — earned only through a genuine track record of verified reviews and sustained trust, and automatically lost if standards slip.

🙋 Two-Way Ratings

Buyers rate sellers, and sellers rate buyers — a rare feature that builds accountability on both sides of every transaction.

💬 Response Time & Reply Rate

Sellers display a live "usually replies within X" badge and response-rate percentage — the same trust signal used by Airbnb and Etsy.

🚩 Instant Reporting

Any buyer can report a seller directly from a product page — the report is logged and simultaneously escalated to admin via WhatsApp for fast action.

🛡️ ZiBuy Protect & Dispute Resolution

An optional buyer-protection fee on checkout requires the buyer to confirm receipt before an order is considered complete — with a built-in dispute system if something goes wrong, reviewed by admin.

Built to turn casual sellers into serious, repeat businesses on ZiBuy

Boosted & Pinned Ads

Platform Control
Admin & Platform Control
A fully built operations console — not just a storefront

ZiBuy is run through a complete internal admin panel that gives full, real-time oversight and control of every part of the platform — built for serious day-to-day operation, not a demo.

👥 Users, Ads & Orders

Full moderation over every user, listing, and order — including ban, suspend, edit, and full account deletion.

🏪 Shops Directory

See every shop on the platform, manually feature any shop on the homepage, or suspend one instantly.

🚨 Live Alerts & Activity Log

A real-time feed of every order, payment, review, and report — plus a full history log of platform-wide activity.

🐛 Developer Error Console

Real client-side errors are captured automatically and surfaced to the admin — issues are visible the moment they happen.

⚠️ Fraud Signal Detection

Automatic flags for sellers with repeated open reports, long-unresolved disputes, or policy inconsistencies.

🛠️ Maintenance Mode

The whole platform can be put into a controlled maintenance state in one click during major updates.

💰 Live-Editable Plan Pricing

Subscription limits and pricing can be changed instantly from the admin panel — no code changes or redeploy required.

📣 Broadcast & Blog System

Push announcements to every user at once, and publish blog content directly from the admin panel.
